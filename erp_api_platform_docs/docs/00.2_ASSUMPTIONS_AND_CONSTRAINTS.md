# Assumptions and Constraints

Status: DRAFT

## Assumptions

- Each customer has an isolated Firebird database.
- Each customer has a distinct Firebird user.
- The API will run in a controlled server environment.
- Firebird databases should remain on a private network.
- A structure-only `.FDB` may be supplied for discovery.
- Database inspection is performed without assuming access to ERP source code.

## Constraints

- Do not expose Firebird directly to the Internet.
- Do not require clients to provide database paths.
- Do not require clients to provide Firebird usernames/passwords.
- Do not use SYSDBA for API runtime access.
- Do not infer undocumented business behavior as fact.
- Do not automatically publish sensitive fields.
- Do not automatically expose triggers/procedures as endpoints.
- Do not change ERP database structures unless explicitly approved.
- Do not claim scalability without benchmark evidence.

## Unknowns

Unknown items must be recorded rather than guessed.

Examples:

- undocumented business rules;
- application-layer behavior absent from the database;
- meaning of legacy fields;
- side effects of procedures;
- operational limits of specific Firebird installations.
